Velocity Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Velocity measures bar speed during strength training using your Apple Watch. We built it to keep your data on your devices.

What Velocity collects

  • Motion data from the Apple Watch (accelerometer and device motion) while a set is being recorded.
  • Set details you enter: lift, athlete names, weight, notes, and numbers from other training devices.

Where it's stored

On your Apple Watch until it's sent to your iPhone, then on your iPhone. Velocity has no accounts and no servers, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

Health

Velocity starts a workout session only so the watch app keeps running while your wrist is down. It does not read or write any data in the Health app, and the workout is not saved.

Sharing

Your data leaves your iPhone only when you choose to export it (for example by Mail or AirDrop). We don't sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Delete sets in Velocity's History, or delete the app to remove everything.
